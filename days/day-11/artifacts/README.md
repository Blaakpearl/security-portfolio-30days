## Key Methodology Notes

- **Confidence standard:** ICD 203 (Intelligence Community Directive 203) language used throughout
- **Central finding:** HIGH confidence that no specific named threat actor can be attributed
- **Profile assessment:** MODERATE confidence — financially motivated, moderately sophisticated actor
- **Explicit non-findings:** Documented what the evidence does NOT support (see REPORT.md)
- **Alternative hypotheses:** 3 competing explanations evaluated with supporting/contradicting evidence

> This lab demonstrates disciplined attribution methodology — resisting the temptation
> to overclaim identity based on weak or circumstantial evidence (timing patterns, TTP
> overlap, single-source geolocation). Commit your real output files after running the
> lab scripts.
